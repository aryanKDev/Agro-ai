SECTION 1: PM-KISAN (PRADHAN MANTRI KISAN SAMMAN NIDHI)

1.1 SCHEME OVERVIEW
PM-KISAN is a Central Sector Scheme providing income support to all land-holding farmer families across India. Launched on 24 February 2019 by the Government of India.

Financial Benefit:
- ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 each
- Transferred directly to farmers' bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Instalment periods: April–July, August–November, December–March

1.2 ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
Who is eligible:
- All land-holding farmer families with cultivable land
- Both small and marginal farmers as well as large farmers are eligible
- Must be Indian citizens with agricultural land holdings in their name
- Land records must be updated in state revenue department

Who is NOT eligible:
- Institutional land holders
- Farmer families holding constitutional posts
- Serving/retired government employees with monthly pension > ₹10,000
- Income Tax payers (with few exceptions)
- Professionals: doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants

1.3 HOW TO REGISTER FOR PM-KISAN
Method 1 — Online Registration:
1. Visit pmkisan.gov.in
2. Click "Farmers Corner" → "New Farmer Registration"
3. Enter Aadhaar number and mobile number
4. Fill in land details and bank account information
5. Submit and note Registration ID

Method 2 — Through Common Service Centre (CSC):
- Visit nearest CSC with Aadhaar card, land records (Khasra/Khatauni), and passbook
- CSC operator will complete registration free of cost

Method 3 — Through Agriculture Department:
- Contact local Patwari or Lekhpal for registration assistance

1.4 HOW TO CHECK PM-KISAN STATUS
- Visit pmkisan.gov.in → "Farmers Corner" → "Beneficiary Status"
- Enter Aadhaar number, Account number, or Mobile number
- Check payment history and next instalment date

1.5 PM-KISAN HELPLINE
- National Helpline: 155261 or 1800115526 (Toll-Free)
- Email: pmkisan-ict@gov.in
- State-wise nodal officers available on the portal

SECTION 2: PMFBY — PRADHAN MANTRI FASAL BIMA YOJANA (CROP INSURANCE)

2.1 SCHEME OVERVIEW
PMFBY provides comprehensive crop insurance coverage against natural calamities, pests and diseases, post-harvest losses, and localized calamities.

2.2 PREMIUM RATES FOR FARMERS
- Kharif Crops: Maximum 2% of sum insured (rest paid by Central + State Govt)
- Rabi Crops: Maximum 1.5% of sum insured
- Commercial/Horticultural Crops: Maximum 5% of sum insured
- Actual actuarial premium is 10–20%; farmer pays only 2% for Kharif

Example: Tomato insured at ₹60,000/hectare
- Farmer premium (5%): ₹3,000/hectare
- Government subsidy: ₹15,000–20,000/hectare (rest of premium)
- In case of 100% crop loss: Farmer receives ₹60,000/hectare

2.3 COVERAGE UNDER PMFBY
- Prevented Sowing/Planting: If crop cannot be sown due to adverse weather
- Standing Crop Loss: Due to drought, flood, hailstorm, cyclone, pest/disease attacks
- Post-Harvest Losses: Up to 14 days for crops kept in field to dry
- Localized Calamities: Hailstorm, landslide, inundation for individual farms

2.4 HOW TO APPLY
- KCC (Kisan Credit Card) holders: Auto-enrollment — opt out if not interested
- Non-KCC farmers: Apply through bank, CSC, or state government portal
- Apply before sowing cut-off date (announced by state government)
- Online: pmfby.gov.in

SECTION 3: SOIL HEALTH CARD (SHC) SCHEME

3.1 SCHEME OVERVIEW
The Soil Health Card scheme provides every farmer a Soil Health Card every 2 years, showing nutrient status of their soil along with recommendations for appropriate dosage of nutrients.

3.2 WHAT IS TESTED
- Macro nutrients: N, P, K
- Secondary nutrients: S (Sulfur), Ca (Calcium), Mg (Magnesium)
- Micro nutrients: Zn, B, Fe, Mn, Cu
- Physical parameters: pH, EC (Electrical Conductivity), Organic Carbon

3.3 HOW TO GET SOIL HEALTH CARD
1. Contact local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Agriculture Department
2. Soil sample will be collected from your field (0–15 cm depth, 4–6 sub-samples per acre)
3. Sample tested at Soil Testing Laboratory (STL)
4. Report generated and available on soilhealth.dac.gov.in within 2–4 weeks
5. Report includes crop-wise fertilizer recommendations

3.4 USING THE SOIL HEALTH CARD
- Follow NPK recommendations on the card exactly — over-application wastes money and harms environment
- Acidic soils: Card will recommend liming (calcium carbonate application)
- Micronutrient deficiency: Card will specify which micronutrients to apply and at what dose

SECTION 4: KISAN CREDIT CARD (KCC) SCHEME

4.1 OVERVIEW
KCC provides farmers with affordable credit for their agricultural operations at reduced interest rates.

4.2 KCC BENEFITS
- Credit limit: Based on land holding and crop production requirements (typically ₹1,00,000–₹3,00,000 for 2–5 acres)
- Interest rate: 9% per annum (reduced to 4% effective with government interest subvention)
- Additional 3% subvention for prompt repayment (effective rate: 4%)
- Valid for 5 years with annual renewal
- Includes insurance coverage for personal accident (₹50,000 death/permanent disability)

4.3 HOW TO APPLY FOR KCC
- Apply at any nationalized bank, cooperative bank, or Regional Rural Bank (RRB)
- Documents required:
  - Aadhaar card
  - Land ownership documents (Khasra/7/12 extract)
  - Passport-size photographs
  - Bank account details

4.4 KCC FOR ANIMAL HUSBANDRY AND FISHERIES
Extended to allied activities:
- Animal husbandry farmers: Credit for feed, medicines, equipment
- Fish farmers: Credit for pond preparation, fingerlings, feed
- Limit: ₹2 lakh for small farmers in allied activities

SECTION 5: NATIONAL AGRICULTURE MARKET (e-NAM)

5.1 OVERVIEW
e-NAM (Electronic National Agriculture Market) is a pan-India electronic trading portal that networks existing APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities.

5.2 BENEFITS FOR FARMERS
- Better price discovery through transparent online bidding
- Reduced dependence on middlemen
- Payment directly to bank account within 24–48 hours
- Access to buyers from across India
- Quality assay (grading) of produce at mandi

5.3 HOW TO REGISTER ON e-NAM
- Register at enam.gov.in or at your nearest e-NAM linked APMC
- Documents: Aadhaar, bank passbook, land records
- After registration, bring produce to e-NAM linked mandi for quality testing and online bidding

5.4 COMMODITIES TRADED ON e-NAM
Over 200 commodities including:
- Cereals: Wheat, Paddy, Maize, Sorghum
- Oilseeds: Soybean, Groundnut, Mustard
- Pulses: Chickpea, Pigeon pea, Moong
- Vegetables: Onion, Potato, Tomato, Garlic
- Fruits: Mango, Banana, Grapes
