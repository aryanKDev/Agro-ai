SAFE PESTICIDE USAGE GUIDE FOR VEGETABLE CROPS — INDIA
=======================================================
Source: Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIB&RC), Government of India
Category: Pesticide Management | Crops: Tomato, Potato, Onion, Brinjal

---

SECTION 1: INSECTICIDE RECOMMENDATIONS

1.1 FRUIT AND SHOOT BORER (Leucinodes orbonalis) — BRINJAL
- Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC: 0.3 mL/L water — most effective, low mammalian toxicity
- Emamectin benzoate 5% SG: 0.4 g/L water — excellent control, 7-day PHI
- Spinosad 45% SC: 0.3 mL/L water — biological origin, safe for beneficial insects
- Indoxacarb 14.5% SC: 0.7 mL/L water — rotate with above for resistance management
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): Minimum 7 days before harvest

1.2 APHIDS AND WHITEFLY
- Imidacloprid 17.8% SL: 0.5 mL/L water — systemic, do not apply during flowering
- Thiamethoxam 25% WG: 0.3 g/L water — highly effective for whitefly; 7-day PHI
- Acetamiprid 20% SP: 0.2 g/L water — less harmful to bees than imidacloprid
- Dimethoate 30% EC: 2.0 mL/L water — broad-spectrum, 15-day PHI
Safety note: Neonicotinoids (Imidacloprid, Thiamethoxam) are harmful to bees — avoid spraying during flowering

1.3 MITES (Tetranychus urticae — Red Spider Mite)
- Abamectin 1.9% EC: 0.5 mL/L — most effective miticide, 3-day PHI
- Propargite 57% EC: 2.0 mL/L water — contact miticide
- Hexythiazox 5.45% EC: 1.0 mL/L water — ovicidal action
- Fenazaquin 10% EC: 2.0 mL/L water

1.4 CUTWORMS AND SOIL PESTS
- Chlorpyrifos 20% EC: 2.5 mL/L water — soil drench or spray
- Fipronil 5% SC: 1.5 mL/L water — soil application for termites and cutworms
- Carbofuran 3% CG: 33 kg/ha — soil granule application at planting (restricted use)

SECTION 2: FUNGICIDE RECOMMENDATIONS

2.1 EARLY BLIGHT CONTROL (Alternaria solani) — TOMATO, POTATO
- Mancozeb 75% WP: 2.5 g/L — broad-spectrum protectant, 7-day PHI
- Chlorothalonil 75% WP: 2.0 g/L — excellent protectant, 7-day PHI
- Azoxystrobin 23% SC: 1.0 mL/L — systemic + protectant, 3-day PHI
- Tebuconazole 25.9% EC: 1.0 mL/L — systemic, 7-day PHI
- Difenoconazole 25% EC: 0.5 mL/L — systemic triazole fungicide

2.2 LATE BLIGHT CONTROL (Phytophthora infestans)
- Metalaxyl-M 4% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold): 2.5 g/L — best for late blight
- Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP: 3.0 g/L
- Dimethomorph 50% WP: 1.0 g/L
- Fosetyl-Al 80% WP: 2.5 g/L

2.3 POWDERY MILDEW CONTROL
- Sulfur 80% WP: 3.0 g/L — most economical, 1-day PHI
- Tebuconazole 25.9% EC: 0.5 mL/L
- Myclobutanil 10% WP: 1.0 g/L
- Trifloxystrobin 25% + Tebuconazole 50% WG: 0.5 g/L

2.4 DAMPING-OFF IN NURSERY (Pythium, Rhizoctonia)
- Carbendazim 50% WP: 2.0 g/L water — soil drench
- Metalaxyl 35% WS: 6 g/kg seed — seed treatment
- Thiram 75% WP: 3.0 g/kg seed — seed treatment (standard practice)

SECTION 3: HERBICIDES FOR WEED CONTROL

3.1 PRE-EMERGENCE HERBICIDES
- Pendimethalin 30% EC: 3.3 mL/L — apply before weed germination, 3–5 days after transplanting
- Oxyfluorfen 23.5% EC: 2.5 mL/L — broad-spectrum pre-emergence

3.2 POST-EMERGENCE HERBICIDES
- Metribuzin 70% WP: 0.35 g/L — selective for tomato, potato (post-emergence)
- Oxyfluorfen 23.5% EC: 1.5 mL/L — directed spray, avoid contact with crop

Manual Weeding: First weeding at 20–25 DAT, second at 40–45 DAT. Two manual weedings reduce yield loss by 80%.

SECTION 4: GENERAL PESTICIDE SAFETY GUIDELINES

4.1 PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
Always wear when applying pesticides:
- Full-sleeve clothing (cotton, not synthetic)
- Rubber gloves (not cloth gloves)
- Face mask or respirator (N95 minimum)
- Protective goggles
- Rubber boots
- Never spray pesticides without PPE — health risk

4.2 MIXING AND SPRAYING SAFETY
- Read label carefully before use — follow all precautions
- Mix pesticides in open, well-ventilated areas
- Never mix pesticides in glass/plastic bottles meant for food/water
- Do not eat, drink, or smoke during application
- Wash hands and face thoroughly after spraying
- Spray early morning (6–9 AM) or late evening — avoid peak heat and wind
- Never spray against the wind
- Discard empty pesticide containers safely — do not reuse for food/water storage

4.3 PRE-HARVEST INTERVALS (PHI) — CRITICAL
The PHI is the minimum number of days between last pesticide application and harvest:

| Pesticide | PHI (Days) |
|-----------|-----------|
| Mancozeb 75% WP | 7 |
| Chlorothalonil 75% WP | 7 |
| Imidacloprid 17.8% SL | 7 |
| Thiamethoxam 25% WG | 7 |
| Emamectin benzoate 5% SG | 7 |
| Chlorpyrifos 20% EC | 15 |
| Dimethoate 30% EC | 15 |
| Abamectin 1.9% EC | 3 |
| Azoxystrobin 23% SC | 3 |
| Spinosad 45% SC | 1 |

NEVER harvest before PHI expires — pesticide residue in food is a serious health risk.

4.4 RESISTANCE MANAGEMENT
Rotate between different chemical groups to prevent pest resistance:
- Group 1 spray: Mancozeb (EBDC fungicide)
- Group 2 spray: Chlorothalonil (chloronitrile)
- Group 3 spray: Azoxystrobin (QoI / strobilurin)
- Never use the same fungicide group more than 2 consecutive sprays

4.5 PESTICIDE STORAGE
- Store in original containers with labels intact
- Keep in locked, dry, cool, well-ventilated room
- Never store pesticides near food, feed, or water sources
- Keep away from children
- Maintain a register of all pesticides stored

---

SECTION 5: BANNED PESTICIDES IN INDIA (as of 2023)
The following pesticides are banned and must NOT be used:
- Monocrotophos (all crops)
- Methyl parathion
- Phorate (partial ban — restricted use only)
- Triazophos on cotton
- DDT (agricultural use completely banned)

Use of banned pesticides is a criminal offence under Insecticides Act 1968 and can result in prosecution.
