FERTILIZER RECOMMENDATION GUIDE FOR MAJOR VEGETABLE CROPS — INDIA
===================================================================
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR), Varanasi
Category: Fertilizer Management | Applicable Crops: Tomato, Potato, Onion, Brinjal, Capsicum

---

SECTION 1: UNDERSTANDING NPK FERTILIZERS

NPK stands for Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K) — the three primary macronutrients essential for crop growth.

Role of Nitrogen (N):
- Promotes vegetative growth, leaf greenness, and protein synthesis
- Deficiency: yellowing of older leaves starting from tips (chlorosis), stunted growth
- Excess: excessive vegetative growth, reduced fruiting, increased disease susceptibility

Role of Phosphorus (P):
- Promotes root development, flowering, fruiting, and seed formation
- Deficiency: purple/reddish discoloration of leaves, poor root development, delayed maturity
- Excess: interferes with zinc and iron uptake

Role of Potassium (K):
- Improves fruit quality, disease resistance, and water-use efficiency
- Deficiency: brown leaf margins (scorching), weak stems, poor fruit quality
- Excess: interferes with calcium and magnesium uptake

SECTION 2: NPK RECOMMENDATIONS BY CROP

2.1 TOMATO (Solanum lycopersicum)
Recommended NPK dose: 120:60:60 kg/hectare (N:P:K)

Application Schedule:
- Basal (at transplanting): Full P (60 kg/ha as SSP), 1/3 N (40 kg/ha as Urea), Full K (60 kg/ha as MOP)
- First top dressing (30 days after transplanting, DAT): 1/3 N (40 kg/ha)
- Second top dressing (60 DAT, before flowering): 1/3 N (40 kg/ha)

Fertilizer product quantities per acre:
- Urea (46% N): 57 kg/acre total (split in 3 equal doses of ~19 kg each)
- Single Super Phosphate/SSP (16% P2O5): 67 kg/acre (basal only)
- Muriate of Potash/MOP (60% K2O): 22 kg/acre (basal only)

Micronutrient supplement for tomato:
- Boron (Borax 10.5%): 10 kg/ha — apply as basal for fruit quality
- Zinc Sulfate (21%): 25 kg/ha — apply as basal; prevents fruit cracking
- Calcium Nitrate foliar spray: 0.5% solution at fruit development stage

2.2 POTATO (Solanum tuberosum)
Recommended NPK dose: 180:90:120 kg/hectare

Application Schedule:
- Basal: Full P, Full K, 1/2 N at planting
- Top dressing: 1/2 N at earthing up (30 DAP)

Per-acre fertilizer quantities:
- Urea: 85 kg/acre (split equally)
- DAP (18:46:0): 44 kg/acre (basal — provides both N and P)
- MOP: 44 kg/acre (basal)

2.3 ONION (Allium cepa)
Recommended NPK dose: 100:50:50 kg/hectare

- Basal: Full P (SSP 56 kg/acre), Full K (MOP 18 kg/acre), 1/3 N (Urea 16 kg/acre)
- 30 DAT: 1/3 N
- 60 DAT: 1/3 N
- Sulfur application: 30 kg/ha as gypsum — improves flavor and bulb quality

2.4 BRINJAL (Solanum melongena)
Recommended NPK dose: 100:50:50 kg/hectare

- Same schedule as tomato
- Additional calcium ammonium nitrate (CAN) foliar spray at 0.5% during fruiting

2.5 CAPSICUM / BELL PEPPER
Recommended NPK dose: 100:80:80 kg/hectare

- Basal: Full P, Full K, 1/3 N
- Top dressings at 20, 40, and 60 DAT with remaining N
- Foliar MgSO4 (magnesium sulfate) at 1% — prevents blossom end rot

SECTION 3: ORGANIC FERTILIZERS AND THEIR ROLE

3.1 FARMYARD MANURE (FYM)
- Apply 20–25 tonnes/hectare (8–10 tonnes/acre) 2–3 weeks before transplanting
- N:P:K content approximately 0.5:0.2:0.5% — improves soil organic matter
- Enhances water retention and microbial activity

3.2 VERMICOMPOST
- Apply 4–5 tonnes/hectare as basal application
- Richer in N (1.5–2.5%), P (0.9–1.7%), and K (1.2–1.5%) than FYM
- Contains plant growth hormones and beneficial microbes

3.3 BIO-FERTILIZERS
- Azospirillum (N-fixing): 5 packets/acre (200 g/packet) — seed treatment or soil application
- PSB Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria: 5 packets/acre — increases P availability by 25–30%
- Mycorrhiza: 5 kg/acre soil application — improves root absorption

3.4 FERTIGATION (Fertilizer through Drip Irrigation)
For drip-irrigated tomato (recommended schedule):

Week 1–2 (establishment): 19:19:19 NPK @ 2.5 kg/acre/week
Week 3–5 (vegetative): 19:19:19 NPK @ 3 kg/acre/week + Calcium Nitrate 2 kg/week
Week 6–8 (flowering): 0:52:34 (MAP+MKP) @ 3 kg/acre/week
Week 9–12 (fruiting): 13:0:45 (SOP) @ 3 kg/acre/week + Calcium Nitrate 2 kg/week
Week 13+ (harvest): 0:0:50 (SOP) @ 2 kg/acre/week for fruit quality

SECTION 4: SOIL TEST-BASED FERTILIZATION

Always conduct soil testing before applying fertilizers. Send samples to nearest Soil Testing Laboratory (STL) or use Soil Health Card (SHC) recommendations.

pH Correction:
- Acidic soil (pH < 5.5): Apply agricultural lime (CaCO3) at 2–4 tonnes/ha
- Alkaline soil (pH > 8.0): Apply gypsum at 2 tonnes/ha + organic matter

Nutrient Deficiency Quick Reference:
- Yellow older leaves, stunted growth → Nitrogen deficiency — apply Urea foliar spray 2%
- Purple leaves, poor root growth → Phosphorus deficiency — apply DAP 100 g/10L water
- Brown leaf edges, weak stems → Potassium deficiency — apply MOP at 20 kg/acre
- Yellow new leaves with green veins → Iron deficiency — apply FeSO4 0.5% foliar spray
- Stunted growing points, dead terminal buds → Boron deficiency — apply borax 0.2% spray
- White striped leaves → Zinc deficiency — apply ZnSO4 0.5% foliar spray

SECTION 5: FERTILIZER SAFETY AND REGULATIONS

Government Regulations:
- Fertilizer (Control) Order (FCO) 1985 governs quality standards
- Never exceed recommended doses — over-fertilization pollutes groundwater
- Maintain 15-day interval between last fertilizer application and harvest
- Store fertilizers separately from pesticides in dry, cool, ventilated conditions

Subsidy Information:
- Urea: 100% price-subsidized under GoI Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) scheme
- P and K fertilizers: Partial subsidy under NBS scheme
- Check current MRP at nearest fertilizer dealer or state agriculture department
